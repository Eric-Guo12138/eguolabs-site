A few useful answers

Before we talk.

Does this replace an engineer or estimator?

No. EGuo Labs prepares a structured first pass. Technical feasibility, engineering decisions, pricing, lead time, compliance and final quotation approval stay with your team.

Does EGuo Labs generate the final quotation?

No. Quote-Ready prepares the requirements, missing inputs, clarification questions and internal brief before detailed quotation work. Your team creates and approves the quotation.

Do you need access to our CRM, ERP or mailbox?

No. The fixed pilot works from redacted RFQs or quotation data supplied by your team.

Can customer information be removed?

Yes. Customer/company names and unnecessary identifiers can be removed before material is shared.

What do you need for the Quote-Ready Sprint?

Up to 10 recent RFQs or technical enquiries, with any relevant drawing, BOM or specification needed for the agreed review.

What happens after the sprint?

You receive the reviewed outputs and a short summary of recurring intake gaps. If the workflow proves useful, a larger implementation can be discussed. There is no obligation to continue.

Can we test one RFQ first?

Yes. Send one redacted RFQ first. I’ll return the Quote-Ready output at no charge. If it is not useful, you do not need to continue with the £300 sprint.
