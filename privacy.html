Your information

Privacy.

A straightforward note on visiting this website and getting in touch.

Website visits

This website does not intentionally use advertising or behavioural tracking cookies. It has no analytics or advertising tracking pixels. The contact form uses Formspree when you submit an enquiry.

The hosting provider may process standard technical request logs needed to operate and secure the website.

Contact form and email enquiries

Eric Guo, trading as EGuo Labs, uses the information you provide to understand your enquiry, respond to you and discuss requested services. The form collects your name, work email, optional company, enquiry category and workflow description, together with a submission timestamp and website source.

When you select “Send Enquiry”, your information is sent over HTTPS to Formspree, our form-processing service. Formspree processes and may store the submission and sends a notification to eric@eguolabs.com. Your email address is supplied for replies. Direct email enquiries are handled through our email provider, Microsoft 365.

Formspree may also process technical data such as IP address, browser information and request details for service operation and spam prevention. Its processing may take place in the United States and other countries where it operates. See Formspree's privacy policy for its data practices.

The website does not save enquiry text in browser storage or publish submissions to GitHub. The information remains in the form if a submission fails so you can correct it or use the direct email option.

Retention and privacy requests

Enquiries may remain in the Formspree submission inbox and our email records. This website does not apply an automatic deletion schedule; provider retention and any correspondence records are handled separately. No fixed deletion period is promised here.

For access, correction or deletion requests, contact eric@eguolabs.com. Eric will review the relevant form and email records and any requirements to retain correspondence.

Technical and confidential information

Please do not send confidential technical information through ordinary email unless an appropriate handling arrangement has been agreed. Initial workflow discussions can use a description of the process without confidential drawings or unnecessary personal information.

Questions

For privacy questions, contact Eric Guo at eric@eguolabs.com.
