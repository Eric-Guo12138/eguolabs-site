01 / Before quotation

Quote-Ready Sprint

Move worthwhile RFQs toward a cleaner quotation review without immediately adding more manual first-pass work.

What you send

Up to 10 redacted RFQs / technical enquiries.

Optional: relevant BOM, drawing or specification.

£300 fixed

48 hours after complete pilot input is received.

No CRM / ERP integration.
No customer-system credentials required.

What you get

For each RFQ:

  • Confirmed requirements
  • Missing / ambiguous information
  • Quote blockers
  • Clarification questions
  • Internal quotation brief
  • Draft customer response
  • Quote-readiness status
  • Recommended next action

Your team’s decision. Final technical feasibility, engineering, pricing, lead time, compliance and quotation approval remain with your team.

02 / After quotation

Quote Recovery Sprint

Separate quotations that genuinely need action from waiting states and opportunities that already have a visible next step.

What you send

20–50 anonymised quotation rows or a limited set of recent quotation threads.

Example fields: quote reference, quote date, value if available, status, last action, owner and next-action note.

£300 fixed

48 hours after complete pilot input is received.

No CRM access required.

What you get

  • Prioritised recovery queue
  • Age / inactivity review
  • Visible-next-action flags
  • Reason each item needs attention
  • Recommended next action
  • Ready-to-send follow-up
  • REVIEW / do-not-chase classification

Review before follow-up. Priorities are based only on the supplied quotation data. Waiting states and incomplete evidence remain for review; commercial judgement stays with your team.
