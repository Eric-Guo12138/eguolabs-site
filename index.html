08 / USEFUL ANSWERS

Before We
Get Started.

Do we need to replace our existing estimating software?+

Usually, no. We build around the tools your team already uses, with the required connections and data exchange agreed for the project.

What types of workflows can you help automate?+

Drawing take-off workflows, pipework schedules, spreadsheet preparation and internal review handoffs. Tell us where the repetitive work happens.

Can you work with our existing Excel or listing format?+

Yes — custom estimating tools can be developed around your column layouts, naming conventions and listing requirements. We agree the output with you.

Do you provide a complete sprinkler estimating platform?+

We develop bespoke workflows and internal tools for specific estimating needs, rather than offering an off-the-shelf, full estimating platform.

How does a bespoke project begin?+

Describe one manual step and the output you need. We'll explore an approach, then agree the scope, review criteria and commercial terms.

Can you work with sensitive project documents?+

Start with a description of the workflow. Before sharing sensitive documents, we'll discuss confidentiality, data-processing requirements and authorised sharing.

Who reviews the technical output?+

Your qualified team reviews technical outputs. We build the relevant checks, exceptions and approval points into the workflow.

Do you provide ongoing support after implementation?+

Support, maintenance, handover and future changes can be included in the project arrangements. We'll agree what your team needs before implementation.
