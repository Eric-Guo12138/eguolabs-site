A few useful answers

Before we talk.

Does this replace an engineer or estimator?

No. The output is a structured commercial first pass. Technical feasibility, engineering, product selection, pricing, lead time, compliance and final quotation approval remain with your team.

Do you need access to our CRM or production email?

Not for an initial pilot. Validation can start with anonymised historical enquiries.

Can it work with custom products rather than a fixed catalogue?

Yes. The output does not need to be a SKU match. It can identify a likely route, highlight missing requirements and prepare the enquiry for engineering review.

What do you need for a pilot?

For Quote-Ready, up to 10 redacted RFQs and any relevant BOM, drawing or specification. For Quote Recovery, 20–50 anonymised quotation rows or a limited set of recent quotation threads. Each pilot is £300 fixed, with a 48-hour turnaround after complete input is received.

What about sensitive customer information?

Use redacted or anonymised material. Confirm any required confidentiality, DPA, retention or deletion process before sending material. Only share information needed for the agreed review.
